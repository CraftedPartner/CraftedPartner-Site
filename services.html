What we do

Three services. No fluff.

Recruiting, fractional HR, and management consulting. No overhead, no jargon.

01
Recruiting

The right hire. Every time.

Hiring the wrong person costs more than the salary. We make sure it doesn't happen.

  • Targeted sourcing
    We don't send piles of resumes. We go find the people who aren't looking yet.
  • Screening and scheduling
    We handle the back-and-forth so you only meet candidates worth your time.
  • Offer through onboarding
    We stay through the finish line so the hire actually sticks.
  • Talent strategy
    Need a repeatable process your team can run? We build that too.
02
Fractional HR

HR that actually protects you.

Most small businesses are one bad termination or unemployment claim away from a real problem. We make sure you're not.

  • Compliance and policy
    Clear, legally sound policies your team will actually use.
  • Corrective action and terminations
    We handle the tough calls the right way.
  • Unemployment claims
    We respond, represent, and win.
  • Performance management
    Systems that hold people accountable without creating chaos.
  • HR audits
    Know exactly where you stand before it becomes a problem.
03
Management Consulting

Fix what's broken. Build what's missing.

If your team is underperforming, your roles are unclear, or your org is outgrowing its own structure, we come in, assess, and fix it.

  • Org design and role clarity
    Everyone knows what they own and what winning looks like.
  • Leadership development
    Practical coaching that changes behavior, not just mindset.
  • Accountability systems
    Scorecards, KPIs, and 1-on-1 structures that actually get used.
  • AI adoption
    Help your team use AI to move faster on things that matter, not just look busy.
  • Operational efficiency
    Find the bottlenecks, fix the process, move on.
