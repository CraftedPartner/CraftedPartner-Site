HR Risk Check

How exposed is your business?

15 quick questions. About 3 minutes. Your score shows up right away, free.

Your time and your team

01Do you spend less than 5 hours a week dealing with people issues?

02Would you happily rehire every person on your team today?

03Does everyone know what “great” looks like in their role, and how they're measured?

04Do you know why people leave, and is turnover under control?

05Can your managers handle tough conversations without pulling you in?

Money and legal exposure

06Is everyone classified correctly (exempt vs. non-exempt, employee vs. contractor)?

07Do you track hours and pay overtime and breaks correctly for hourly employees?

08Do you follow a consistent termination process, including paying final wages on time?

09Do managers document performance issues and corrective action in writing?

10Do you respond to every unemployment claim, with documentation?

11Do you have a written anti-harassment policy, a way to report issues, and training?

Paperwork that protects you

12Has your employee handbook been updated in the last 12 months?

13Are your paid sick leave and other leave policies current with state and federal law?

14Is every employee's I-9 complete and stored correctly?

15Does every employee have a signed offer letter and a current job description?
