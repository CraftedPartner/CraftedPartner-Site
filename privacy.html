Privacy

Privacy policy.

Plain English. Last updated November 15, 2026.

Crafted Consulting (“Crafted,” “we”) runs craftedconsultingusa.com. This page explains what information we collect on this site, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you fill out a form, we get what you type: usually your name, email, phone number, company, and a note about what you need. If you take the HR Risk Check and ask for a review, we also get your answers and score.
  • Newsletter sign-ups. Your email address, so we can send the Crafted Monthly Newsletter.
  • How the site is used. We use Google Analytics to see things like which pages get visited, how people find us, and which buttons get clicked. This uses cookies and collects general info like your browser, device, and rough location. It does not tell us who you are.
  • Ads. We advertise on Google and other platforms. Those platforms may use cookies to show our ads and tell us whether an ad led to a visit or a message.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and do the work you ask us to do.
  • To send the newsletter, if you signed up.
  • To understand what’s working on the site and improve it.

We don’t sell your information. We don’t share it with anyone except the services that help us run the site, listed below.

Services we use

  • Formspree delivers form messages to our inbox.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads measure site traffic and ad results.
  • Calendly handles meeting bookings when you click “Book a time.”
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

Each of these has its own privacy policy that covers the information it handles.

Your choices

  • Newsletter: unsubscribe anytime with the link in any email.
  • Cookies: you can block or delete them in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Your info: email us and we’ll tell you what we have, fix it, or delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and contact info as long as we need them to work with you or follow up, and then we delete what we no longer need.

Kids

This site is for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

Email contact@craftedconsultingusa.com or call 720.656.0230.
